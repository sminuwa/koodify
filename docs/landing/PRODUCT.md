# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: product and engineering leads at fintechs, marketplaces, and platforms who are evaluating or building payment infrastructure and need a dependable way to collect, route, and reconcile money movement without stitching providers ad hoc.

Secondary: developers integrating payment flows into production applications.

## Product Purpose

Koodify provides payment infrastructure so product teams can collect, orchestrate, reconcile, and deliver payment events through one developer-friendly platform. Success means teams ship payment features with less integration overhead and clearer operational visibility across the payment lifecycle.

## Positioning

One integration layer for modern payments: collect, orchestrate, deliver (webhooks), and reconcile—so applications stay decoupled from provider-specific behaviour while keeping a consistent API and event surface.

## Operating Context

Evaluated via a marketing landing page and sales conversation (`hello@koodify.ng`). Integration is API- and webhook-oriented (illustrative JavaScript snippets on the site). Currency and market cues are Nigeria-facing (₦ / NGN). Dashboard and payment UIs shown publicly are illustrative mockups, not live product data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed narrative capabilities on the public site: payment collection / virtual accounts, orchestration across providers, webhook delivery and verification, reconciliation / settlement visibility.

Undecided / not to invent: exact public SDK syntax, production docs URLs, sign-in URLs, compliance certifications, pricing, customer logos, and generally-available capability claims beyond the approved marketing narrative.

Stack: existing static HTML, CSS, and JS landing page; preserve that delivery form unless the user changes it.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity over category costume — infrastructure credibility comes from clear flows and honest claims, not fintech clichés.
2. Developer respect — APIs and events are part of the product story; keep them accurate-to-intent and clearly illustrative when synthetic.
3. One layer, full lifecycle — collect → orchestrate → deliver → reconcile stays the spine of the narrative.
4. Nigeria-rooted, globally legible — local currency cues are fine; the product reads as serious infrastructure, not a local novelty.
5. No invented proof — when evidence is missing, design without fake logos, stats-as-facts, or certifications.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard-accessible navigation for the marketing site. No product-specific assistive-tech requirements beyond standard web accessibility were established.
